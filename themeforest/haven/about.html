Our story

A local agency built on trust

Haven was founded in 2011 with one idea: buying or selling a home should feel personal, not like a transaction. We are a small team of licensed agents and valuers who know every street of our city.

We take on fewer properties than big chains – so every client gets real attention, honest advice and a direct line to the agent who knows their home.
