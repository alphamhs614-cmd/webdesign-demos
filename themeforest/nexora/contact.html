FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Contact us

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The Starter plan is free forever and includes 1,000 tasks per month. Paid plans come with a 14-day free trial – no credit card required.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Describe what you want in plain English or start from one of 300+ templates. Developers can still use the API, webhooks and custom code steps.

Where is my data stored?

In ISO 27001 certified data centres in the EU (Frankfurt) by default. US hosting is available on Business plans. We never train AI models on your data.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel or downgrade in one click from your billing settings. Yearly plans are refunded pro rata.

Do you offer discounts?

Startups, non-profits and education get 50% off the Pro plan. Contact us with a short note about your project.
