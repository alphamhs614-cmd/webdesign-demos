About us

We build tools that give teams their time back.

Nexora started with a simple belief: people should spend their days on creative, meaningful work – and let software handle the rest.

Our story

From 40 scripts to one platform

In 2022 our founders were running operations at a fast-growing startup. Every new tool meant another integration, another script and another thing that broke at 2 a.m.

They built Nexora to connect everything in one place – and to make automation something anyone on the team can set up, not just engineers. Today more than 12,000 teams use Nexora to automate millions of tasks every week.

Values

What we believe in

01

Clarity over complexity

Every feature has to make a workflow easier to understand – not just more powerful.

02

Humans stay in control

AI suggests, people approve. Every automated step is logged and reversible.

03

Privacy by default

We never train models on customer data, and data residency is a setting, not a sales call.

04

Ship small, ship often

We release improvements every week and publish what changed in our changelog.
