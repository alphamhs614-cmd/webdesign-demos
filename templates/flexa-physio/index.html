FAQ

Frequently asked

Can't find your question? Call or message us.

Do I need a prescription?

For statutory insurance yes. Self-payers can come directly for many treatments (in Germany with a limited practitioner permit for physiotherapy, otherwise a short medical clearance is needed).

What should I bring?

Your prescription, insurance card, any reports or scans, comfortable clothes and a towel.

How long does a session take?

Usually 25–45 minutes. The first assessment takes about 60 minutes.

Do you offer home visits?

Yes, for patients who cannot come to the practice – a home-visit prescription is required.

Is the practice accessible?

Yes – step-free entrance, lift and an accessible restroom.
