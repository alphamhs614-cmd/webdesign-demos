FAQ

Good to know

How far in advance should I book?

Portrait sessions are usually available within 2–3 weeks. Weddings and campaigns: 3–9 months ahead.

Do you travel?

Yes – anywhere. Within 50 km of the studio travel is included, beyond that it is charged at cost.

When do I get my images?

Portraits within 5 working days, brand shoots within 72 hours (express) or 7 days, full days within 3 weeks.

Can I use the photos commercially?

The Brand and Full-day packages include a commercial license. Portrait sessions include personal use – an upgrade is possible.

What if it rains?

Outdoor shoots can be moved free of charge – or we use the weather for moody images.
