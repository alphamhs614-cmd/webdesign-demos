Do I need to be at home?

No. Many clients leave a key or code. Your cleaner is vetted and insured, and we confirm every visit by message.

Do I need to provide cleaning products?

No, we bring professional, eco-friendly products and equipment. If you prefer your own products, just tell us.

How is the price calculated?

By service type, number of rooms, frequency and extras. The calculator shows a reliable estimate; we confirm the final price before the first visit.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes, free of charge up to 24 hours before your appointment.
