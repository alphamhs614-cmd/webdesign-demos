FAQ

Good to know

Do you accept new patients?

Yes – new patients are always welcome. You can usually get an appointment within a few days, emergencies on the same day.

I am afraid of the dentist. Can you help?

Absolutely. Tell us when you book. We take extra time, explain every step, agree on a stop signal and offer gentle sedation if needed.

Which insurances do you accept?

We treat publicly and privately insured patients as well as self-payers. Before any treatment you get a clear overview of the costs.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Your insurance card, a list of medication you take, and previous X-rays if you have them.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes, for larger treatments such as implants or aligners we offer interest-free payment plans.
