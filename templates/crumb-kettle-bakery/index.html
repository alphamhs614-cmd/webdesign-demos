Our story

Flour, water, salt – and time

We started with one wood-fired oven and a sourdough starter called Herbert. Today we bake every night for the neighbourhood, with organic flour from regional mills and no shortcuts.

What we don't sell by closing time goes to a local food-sharing initiative.

FAQ

Good to know

Anything else? Just send us a message.

Do you have vegan or gluten-free options?

Yes – several breads and cakes are vegan (marked VG). We are not a gluten-free bakery, but we always have a buckwheat loaf on Thursday. Please ask about allergens.

Until when can I pre-order?

Orders received by 8 pm are ready the next morning from 7 am. Celebration cakes need 48 hours.

Do you deliver?

We do not deliver ourselves, but local offices can order breakfast boxes for pickup. Ask us for a quote.

Can I work from the café?

Of course – there is Wi-Fi and a few power sockets. On weekends we ask laptop users to share tables.

Do you offer wholesale?

We supply a few local cafés and restaurants. Write us for wholesale prices.
