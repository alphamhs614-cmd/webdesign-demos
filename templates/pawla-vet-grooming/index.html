FAQ

Good to know

Still have questions? Send us a message.

Do you treat emergencies?

Yes. During opening hours we see emergencies immediately. Outside opening hours call our 24/7 number – we will guide you.

What should I bring to the first visit?

Your pet's vaccination record or passport, previous reports and – for cats – a secure carrier.

How long does grooming take?

Usually 1.5–3 hours depending on size and coat. We message you when your dog is ready.

Can I pay in instalments?

For larger treatments we offer payment plans. We also work with pet insurance providers.

Do you make house calls?

Yes, for euthanasia, elderly pets and multi-pet households. Ask us about availability.
