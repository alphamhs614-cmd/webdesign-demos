FAQ

Questions?

I am a complete beginner. Is Pulse for me?

Absolutely. Every class is scalable and every new member gets a free onboarding session with a coach.

Can I pause or cancel my membership?

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled with 30 days notice, and you can pause up to 3 months per year.

What do I need for my trial?

Training clothes, clean indoor shoes, a towel and a water bottle. Lockers and showers are available.

Is there parking?

Yes, free parking right in front of the gym and secure bike racks.
