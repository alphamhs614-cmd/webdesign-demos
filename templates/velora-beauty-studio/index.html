FAQ

Good to know

How early should I book?

Popular slots on Fridays and Saturdays are often booked a week in advance. During the week we can usually offer an appointment within 2–3 days.

What if I need to cancel?

No problem – cancel or move your appointment free of charge up to 24 hours before. Later cancellations may be charged at 50 %.

Which products do you use?

Vegan, cruelty-free professional skin care. We are happy to recommend a home-care routine, but there is never any pressure to buy.

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. Every first visit starts with a free skin or hair consultation so we can plan the right treatment for you.

Do you offer gift vouchers?

Yes, for any amount or a specific treatment – as a printed card or as an instant PDF.
