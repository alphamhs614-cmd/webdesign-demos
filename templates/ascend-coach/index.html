FAQ

Questions, answered

Who is coaching for?

For managers, professionals and founders who want to grow, change direction or lead with more confidence – and are ready to put in the work.

How do sessions work?

Sessions take place via video call (Zoom, Teams or Google Meet). You get a short summary and next steps after every session.

What happens in the free call?

We talk about where you are and where you want to go. If we are a good fit, I recommend a program – if not, I will point you to better resources.

Can my employer pay?

Yes. Many clients use their learning budget. I issue proper invoices and can send a short proposal for your manager.

What is your cancellation policy?

Sessions can be moved free of charge up to 24 hours in advance.
