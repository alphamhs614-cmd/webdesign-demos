The studio

From a lump of clay to your table

Kora is a two-person studio. Every piece passes through our hands at least seven times before it reaches you.

  1. Throwing

    Each piece is shaped on the wheel from regional stoneware clay.

  2. Trimming & drying

    We refine the foot and handles, then let everything dry slowly for a week.

  3. Glazing & firing

    Dipped in our own glazes and fired twice – up to 1,240 °C.

FAQ

Questions & answers

More questions? Write us – we usually reply the same day.

How long does shipping take?

In-stock pieces ship within 2 working days. Delivery takes 2–4 days within Germany. Shipping is €5.90 and free from €60.

Can I return an item?

Yes, you can return unused items within 30 days. Please see our cancellation policy for details.

Are the ceramics dishwasher-safe?

All tableware is food- and dishwasher-safe. To keep the glaze beautiful for longer, we recommend hand-washing.

How do I pay?

After you send your order we confirm availability and send a secure payment link (card, PayPal, bank transfer).

Do you take custom orders?

For cafés, restaurants and weddings we make custom sets. Ask for a quote with quantities and dates.
