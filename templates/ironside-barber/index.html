FAQ

Before you sit down

Anything else? Drop us a WhatsApp.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, whenever a chair is free. Book online to skip the wait – especially on Fridays and Saturdays.

How long before should I cancel?

Please cancel at least 12 hours before so someone else can take the slot.

Can I pay by card?

Yes – card, Apple Pay, Google Pay and cash.

Do you cut long hair or curly hair?

Absolutely. Mention it in your booking so we can plan a little more time.

Do you sell gift vouchers?

Yes, in the shop or via WhatsApp – any amount or a specific service.
