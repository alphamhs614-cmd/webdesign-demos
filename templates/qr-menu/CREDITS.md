# Credits – QR Digital Menu (Arabic + German)

## Images
No photos are used. The logo and all 16 dish illustrations in `assets/img/` are original flat SVG drawings made for this template (covered by the template license). Replace them with your own photos any time (JPG/WebP/PNG/SVG, ideally square or 4:3, ~600 px).

## Fonts (SIL Open Font License 1.1, self-hosted in assets/fonts)
- Cairo (Arabic + Latin, incl. German umlauts) – https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Cairo
