Good to know

Quick answers

Still not sure? Call us on 0114 399 2978 or email enquiries@portablestage.co.uk.

How heavy are the platforms?

A PLATINUM 1×1m platform weighs 7.8 kg and a 1×520mm platform 4.2 kg. SteelCity modules weigh 8.5 kg. Every platform takes a load weight of 1500 kg.

Which stage heights are available?

200mm, 300mm, 400mm, 600mm, 800mm and 1000mm, using our concertina riser legs with magnetic connections.

Why magnetic instead of locks?

Other branded platforms have up to 8 locks each that wear out and need replacing. NexGen PLATINUM has no locks or keys, so there is nothing to replace – and platforms last up to 15 years.

Do schools and charities get a discount?

Yes. Education, Charity, Arts & Leisure organisations receive ECAL funding of 10% to 20% discount. Ask for your code when you request a quote.

How much space does storage take?

A 6×3m stage (18 modules) stores on ONE trolley thanks to concertina storage.

Which finishes can I choose?

Platforms are available in carpet or ABS tough black finish, with valance skirting, steps, handrails, ramps and backdrops available as accessories.

Ready to raise your stage?

Tell us your size and venue and we'll send a quote – with ECAL funding of 10–20% applied for schools, charities, arts & leisure organisations.
